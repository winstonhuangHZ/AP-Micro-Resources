# AP Microeconomics 36-Session Schedule

Default format: 18 weeks, two 90-minute meetings per week. Every session has a student-visible product and an objective mapped to the College Board CED.

## Fixed Unit cycle

1. Preview terms and one core graph.
2. Teach the model and its assumptions.
3. Practice numerical, graphing, and written-response forms.
4. Use a mixed quiz or a timed FRQ subpart.
5. Compare work with scoring guidance and record the error type.
6. Reattempt a similar item one week later.
